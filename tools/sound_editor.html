Advanced Sound Parameters

Vibrato changes oscillator pitch or noise-filter frequency.

Noise Filter

Noise filters shape the sound of scratches, hisses, footsteps, and other non-tonal effects.

Volume Envelope

Attack controls the beginning. Sustain controls the middle volume. Release controls the ending.

Rhythmic Repetition

Repeat Count: number of individual sounds. Repeat Interval: seconds between the starts of sounds. Timing Variation: randomizes the spacing to create natural footsteps, wingbeats, or scampering. A count of 1 disables repetition.
